Multiwarden

Every unlocked Bitwarden account, working at once.

Download View source
Experimental · Chrome, Edge, Opera, Firefox and Safari

What it does

Autofill from every account

Inline suggestions come from all unlocked accounts, each labelled with its own.

Save to the right vault

Pick the account for new logins. Multiwarden remembers your choice per site.

Passkeys everywhere

Sign in with a passkey from any account, and choose where new ones are kept.

Combined view

See every vault in one list, with each item marked by its account.

Install

  1. Download the package for your browser and unzip it.
  2. Chromium browsers: open the extensions page, turn on Developer mode and choose Load unpacked. Firefox: open about:debugging and choose Load Temporary Add-on.
  3. Disable the official Bitwarden extension, then sign in to and unlock your accounts.

Available for Chrome, Vivaldi, Brave, Edge, Opera and Firefox. Safari has an unsigned test build for macOS.
